1. `weeks = week_index(cfg)`.
2. `baseline = baseline_demand(cfg, weeks)` — gives `trend`, `season`, `promo_mult`, `promo_flag`, `base`.
3. `spend = generate_spend(cfg, rng, weeks)` — consumes the generator's first six vectors.

6. `revenue_pre_clip = base + sum(m_c) + eps`; `revenue = np.maximum(revenue_pre_clip, 0.0)`. Keep both columns in `components` — SIM-072 checks the pre-clip series, and the clip must never bind in practice.
