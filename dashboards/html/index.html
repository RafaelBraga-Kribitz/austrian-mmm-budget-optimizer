Response curves

Wo die Saettigung beginnt

One curve per channel: weekly contribution against weekly spend at the steady state of a constant weekly spend. The blue rule marks the channel's current weekly spend; the shaded zone lies beyond the largest observed week, where the curve is an extrapolation. Each curve ends at 1.5 times the largest observed weekly spend. Each panel has its own axes: compare shapes across channels, read levels from the axis.
